Home Workouts

No equipment required – follow structured routines at home

💪 Strength (20 min)

  • Push-ups – 3 sets of 12
  • Bodyweight Squats – 3 sets of 15
  • Lunges – 3 sets of 12 per leg
  • Plank – 3 rounds of 45 sec

Tip: Rest 60s between sets. Maintain form, not speed.

🏃 Cardio Blast (25 min)

  • Jump Rope (or simulated) – 3 min
  • High Knees – 3x40 sec
  • Burpees – 3x12
  • Mountain Climbers – 3x20

Tip: Keep water nearby. Do in a ventilated room.

🧘 Yoga Flow (30 min)

  • Sun Salutation – 5 rounds
  • Tree Pose – 30 sec per leg
  • Child Pose – 2 min
  • Warrior Pose – 1 min each side

Tip: Do barefoot, use yoga mat. Focus on breathing.

⚡ HIIT (15 min)

  • Jump Squats – 30 sec
  • Push-ups – 30 sec
  • Burpees – 30 sec
  • Plank – 30 sec
  • ❌ Rest – 30 sec
  • Repeat for 4 rounds

Tip: Give 100% effort in work intervals, rest properly in breaks.
